Privacy

What we collect, and what we do with it

This site does not track you. There are no cookies, no analytics, and no advertising. The only personal information we hold is what you choose to send us.

Last updated August 19, 2026

This policy covers harboraiconsulting.com and the email and calendar contact that follows from it. Harbor AI Consulting is a small consulting business based in New Jersey, United States. Throughout this policy, "we" and "Harbor" mean Harbor AI Consulting, and "you" means anyone visiting the site or contacting us through it.

We have written this in plain language on purpose. If anything here is unclear, email quinnabe@harboraiconsulting.com and ask. We will answer.

1. What we collect

When you fill in the contact form

The form asks for five things. Three are required, two are optional:

  • Your name — required.
  • Your email address — required.
  • Your business name — optional.
  • Your team size — optional, chosen from a list of ranges.
  • Your message, answering what takes up the most time right now — required.

The form is handled by Formspree, which delivers it to us by email. Formspree also records technical details of the submission automatically, including your IP address, browser type, the time you submitted, and the page you came from. We do not use those technical details for anything. They exist so Formspree can block spam and keep its service running.

Please do not put confidential business information, financial account details, health information, or anyone else's personal data into the message box. A first message only needs enough for us to understand the problem and reply.

When you book a discovery call

The booking buttons open Cal.com in a new tab. That booking happens on Cal.com's site, under Cal.com's privacy policy, not ours. What reaches us is your name, your email address, the time you picked, and anything you typed into the booking form.

When you email us directly

We receive the message, your email address, your name if your account shows one, and whatever you write or attach. Our email runs on Google Workspace.

When you only visit the site

You do not have to give us anything to read this site, and we do not ask. Three technical things still happen, because they are how any website is delivered:

  • Our host sees your request. The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare processes your IP address and request details in order to serve the page and to protect the site from attacks.
  • Google receives your IP address for fonts. The typefaces load from Google Fonts, so your browser requests those files from Google's servers.
  • The styling library loads from a public CDN. The site's stylesheet framework is served from a content delivery network, which likewise sees the request.

We do not receive, store, or look at any of that. It is not connected to your name and we have no way to identify you from it.

2. What we do not do

This is a short list because the site is deliberately simple. All of the following are true today, and you can confirm most of them in your browser's developer tools:

  • No cookies. This site sets none at all, so there is no cookie banner to click.
  • No analytics. We do not run Google Analytics or any other visitor measurement tool. We genuinely do not know how many people visit.
  • No tracking pixels, no advertising networks, no retargeting. Nothing follows you to another site.
  • No selling or renting your information. Not to data brokers, not to anyone, not for any price.
  • No newsletter you did not ask for. Sending us a message does not add you to a mailing list.
  • No automated decisions about you. A person reads every message.

If this changes, this page changes first. If we ever add analytics, we will update this policy and the date at the top of it before turning anything on.

3. Why we hold it

We use what you send for three things, and nothing else:

  • To reply to you. This is the main one. You asked a question; we answer it.
  • To prepare for a call. If we are speaking, we read what you wrote beforehand so you do not have to repeat it.
  • To keep our own business records. Who we spoke to, what was proposed, what was agreed.

If you become a client, we also hold the working information the engagement needs, and the billing information required to invoice you. That is described in the agreement you sign, which sits alongside this policy.

4. Who else can see it

We are a small business and we use ordinary business software. Those providers can technically access the data they process for us. We do not give your information to anyone else.

The one other case where information could leave us is a legal one: if a court order, subpoena, or similar legal obligation required us to hand something over. That has never happened. If it does, and we are permitted to tell you, we will.

5. How long we keep it

  • If we never do business together — we keep the exchange for up to two years, in case you come back. Then we delete it. If you would rather we delete it now, ask and we will.
  • If you become a client — we keep engagement records for seven years after the work ends. That is the ordinary period for business, tax, and professional records.

Our providers keep their own copies on their own schedules, which are set out in their policies rather than this one.

6. Your choices

You can ask us to do any of the following, at any time, and we will do it within 30 days:

  • Tell you what we hold about you.
  • Correct it if it is wrong.
  • Delete it. We will, unless we are legally required to keep a record — for example a paid invoice.
  • Stop contacting you. Say the word and we stop.

Email quinnabe@harboraiconsulting.com and say which one you want. There is no form to fill in, and we will not charge you or ask you to justify the request.

To be straightforward about the legal position: Harbor is a small business and is below the size threshold at which New Jersey's privacy law and the comparable laws in other states apply to us. We offer the choices above because they are the right way to treat people, not because a statute currently forces us to. That is a promise we are making to you on this page, and we will keep it whether or not the law catches up with our size.

7. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Our accounts use strong, unique passwords and two-factor authentication. Only one person, Harbor's owner, has access to enquiries.

We will not tell you your data is perfectly safe, because no business can honestly say that. Most of what we hold sits on servers run by the providers listed above, and their security is theirs to maintain. What we can commit to is this: if information you gave us is exposed, we will tell you what happened and when, without waiting to be asked.

8. Children

This site sells consulting services to businesses. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has sent us information, email us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and change the date at the top. If the change is significant and we already hold your email address, we will email you rather than rely on you noticing.
